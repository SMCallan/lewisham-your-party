The votes are in, and Lewisham is now an official branch of Your Party.

Thank you to everyone who volunteered to stand, everyone who voted, and everyone who has helped get the branch off the ground. This is a huge step for organising in our borough.

## Your Elected Branch Officers

- **Chair:** Choo-Chen Sim
- **Secretary:** Daniel Ansell
- **Treasurer:** Callan MacDonald
- **Organiser:** John McGrath
- **Workplace Liaison:** Callum Carter

You can also find the officers on our [Who We Are](/about) page.

## What's Next?

That's up to you. At our next branch meeting, members will set the branch's priorities: bringing members together, organising in our communities, developing local leadership and much more.

Keep an eye on your emails and our [Events](/events) page in the coming weeks for details.

Congratulations Lewisham. Now let's get going!
