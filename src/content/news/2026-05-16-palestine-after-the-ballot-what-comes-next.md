# Palestine After the Ballot — What Comes Next?

Join us on **Thursday 21 May at 6:30pm** for a workshop-style discussion on Palestine, political organising, and what comes next after the ballot.  

We’ll be joined by **Green Party activist Lubna Speitan** and **lawyer Franck Magennis** for a discussion on how we can collectively challenge the British state as it continues to participate in the genocide of Palestinians, and how we keep Palestine at the heart of our organising locally, nationally and internationally.  

This will be an open, participatory meeting with space for discussion, breakout groups, feedback, and practical next steps. Together, we’ll consider what Lewisham Your Party can do next — from local campaigning and solidarity work to future meetings, community organising, youth work, schools, health and social care, local government, anti-imperialism, and more.
